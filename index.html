About Me

Senior ERPNext Developer and Consultant based in Yanbu, Saudi Arabia. I lead the full-time, on-site ERPNext program at FMH Marine Services, a Saudi ship and port agency. I designed and built the system from scratch, from the first requirement through ship-agency operations and finance, including the Fasah Pay integration and consulting on finance processes and workflow design.

Before FMH I worked as a Senior ERP Consultant at Asl Al-Uroba, and across earlier roles I delivered ERPNext and Django systems for government, enterprise, logistics, and education. That work covers system architecture, backend engineering, data migration, performance on large datasets, and complex domain modules.

I care about clean architecture, reliable operations, and ERP systems that follow how the business actually runs.
